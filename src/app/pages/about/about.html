A Cup That Started It All

It all began with a simple dream. In 2018, our founder Maria Santos, a coffee enthusiast from Davao, opened a small café serving freshly brewed coffee to students and professionals in the community.

What started as a small neighborhood café quickly grew through word of mouth. Today, Bean & Brew has expanded to multiple branches across Mindanao, serving thousands of happy customers every day.

We proudly embrace Filipino coffee culture — from bold Kapeng Barako to modern café favorites — while creating a warm and welcoming space for everyone.
