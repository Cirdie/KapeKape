Our Story

Brewed with Passion,
Shared with Community

Founded in 2018 in Tagum City, Bean & Brew began as a small neighborhood café built on a love for quality coffee and genuine Filipino hospitality. What started as a humble coffee corner has grown into a welcoming space for students, professionals, families, and friends.

We proudly serve a mix of locally inspired coffee favorites and modern café classics, using carefully selected beans and fresh ingredients to make every cup memorable.
